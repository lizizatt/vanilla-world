Original chunky low-poly fantasy racer, built entirely from procedural geometry and flat-color materials without external art, textures, or game files. Each embedded GLB contains **5,427 triangles**: `dug-racer.glb` (`dug_default`, ochre/brass), `dug-racer-green.glb` (`dug_goblin_green`), and `dug-racer-crimson.glb` (`dug_crimson`). All variants use meters, +Y up, forward -Z, and a ground-level origin centered between the engines; dimensions are approximately 5.4 m wide, 10.7 m long, and 2.4 m tall. Long tapered engines lead with angular slanted vent cowls and a jade emissive arc; the small cockpit center trails the engine rears by 4.51 m toward +Z, with 3.90 m clear longitudinal separation and thin, drooping tow lines running backward from the inboard rear cross-brace. The chair faces forward toward the engines. Named group nodes are `Body`, `EngineL`, `EngineR`, and `Cockpit`, whose origin is at the seat cushion for camera attachment; `ExhaustL` and `ExhaustR` remain empty attachment nodes at the rear nozzle mouths (exhaust travels toward +Z). Pod and cables leave a 0.6 m radius corridor clear behind each nozzle. Regenerate with Blender 2.90 using `blender -b --factory-startup -t 2 --python-exit-code 1 -P tools\vehicles\make_dug_racer.py`; the script re-imports every export and asserts node names, attachment types, triangle budget, scale, ground height, cockpit clearance, engine separation, tow-line length/angle, seat attachment and facing, and exhaust clearance. Optionally append `-- --preview <path.png>` to render three-quarter, overhead (`<path>-above.png`), and side (`<path>-side.png`) previews outside the repository.
