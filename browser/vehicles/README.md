Original chunky low-poly fantasy racer with embedded procedural 128x128 painted textures, diffuse colors, roughness 1 and metallic 0; no external art, textures, or game files are used. Each GLB contains **5,297 triangles**: `dug-racer.glb` (`dug_default`, saturated red), `dug-racer-green.glb` (`dug_goblin_green`), and `dug-racer-crimson.glb` (`dug_crimson`). Thick textured hemp bindings and tow ropes, dark riveted rear collars, an iron-banded wooden pod with rimmed discs, original chalk grin markings on the angular cowls, and a subtle jade arc provide a painted fantasy look while preserving the previous silhouette. All variants use meters, +Y up, forward -Z, and a ground-level origin centered between the engines; dimensions remain approximately 5.4 m wide, 10.7 m long, and 2.4 m tall. The small cockpit center trails the engine rears by 4.51 m toward +Z with 3.90 m clear longitudinal separation, and its chair faces forward toward the engines. Named group nodes are `Body`, `EngineL`, `EngineR`, and `Cockpit`, whose origin is at the seat cushion for camera attachment; `ExhaustL` and `ExhaustR` are empty rear nozzle attachments (flames travel +Z), with a 0.6 m radius corridor clear of pod and ropes. Regenerate using Blender 2.90: `blender -b --factory-startup -t 2 --python-exit-code 1 -P tools\vehicles\make_dug_racer.py`; re-import checks cover geometry, attachment names, scale, ground height, trailing seat and facing, rope length/angle, flame clearance, UVs, embedded texture dimensions, and diffuse material settings.
