# Wishlist

Ideas the user wants eventually. Nothing here is started.

## Azeroth interior ("The Core")

Paused before any work was done.

- Free-fly camera noclip: no collision with terrain, water or anything else (drive and podracer collision unchanged).
- Concentric interior layers, each with its own shader, stylised after the film The Core rather than realistic:
  - crust, empty inside, with broken geometry left visible when seen from beneath;
  - mantle;
  - outer core, liquid and translucent enough that the inner core's silhouette can just be made out from the edge of the mantle;
  - inner core, a solid ball of red-hot iron. The outer core's silhouette should likewise be just discernible from the edge of the mantle.
- Continuous transition by depth, as with ascending to space: same frame of reference, no loading screen, no body swap or hot load like the Elune swap. A podracer should eventually be able to cross it seamlessly.
- Later: a custom island with a tunnel down to the core. Dial in the core environment first.
- Tooling idea: poses inside the planet round-trip, teleports to each layer, tuning sliders.
