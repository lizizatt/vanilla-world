Pick the Data folder of your Classic 1.12 install. Files stay on your computer and are never uploaded.

An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment. It contains no game files: you must supply your own legally obtained game client. Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge) are recommended; Firefox must re-pick the folder on every load.
