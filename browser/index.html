Pick the Data folder of your Classic 1.12 install. Files stay on your computer and are never uploaded.

Where to get the Data files

WoW: the Classic 1.12 (vanilla) client can be found online; use its Data folder.

Racer: buy STAR WARS Episode I: Racer on Steam, then right-click it in your library, Manage, Browse local files, and pick the data folder inside. Browsers may refuse Program Files, so copy that folder elsewhere (for example to your Documents) and pick the copy.

An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment. It contains no game files: you must supply your own legally obtained game client. Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge) are recommended; Firefox must re-pick the folder on every load.

    Handling

    Driving input is ignored while the menu is open.

    W/S thrust/reverse · A/D steer · Up/Down pod pitch · X brake · J hop · Shift boost · Space/C drift · F9 reset.

    Camera: F cockpit · left-drag orbit · right-drag steer · Left/Right arrows look · wheel chase zoom · V look back. Pod pitch: Down arrow/stick raises the nose; Up lowers it, up to 20°, returning level on release. Flight: W/S forward/back · A/D strafe · Q/E altitude · arrows look · Shift fast · X look straight down at the point below (the globe ring is where you land). Shortcuts: M map · T track editor · N night · Esc this menu · F near a race flag starts the race.

    Pod controller: left stick steer/pitch · RT accelerate · LT brake/reverse thrust · bottom face (PS X / Xbox A) boost · left face (square / Xbox X) drift · RB/R1 hop · right stick look/click cockpit · D-pad chase zoom · Y/triangle reset · Start fly/drive · Back menu.

    Pod in space: hold right mouse and drag to aim the camera, or hold LB/L1 and use the right stick. The pod smoothly turns toward the camera centre; thrust follows its nose. Release to keep the current attitude and smoothly return the camera to follow. Available during inertial flight outside the atmosphere, not on the ground.
